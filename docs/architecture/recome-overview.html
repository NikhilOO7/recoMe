The thesis

The chat is not the product. The personal interest graph is.

Perplexity searches the world. Recome searches your past attention. Two categorically different products powered by two categorically different data sources — one of which we own and they can’t replicate.

Capture from anywhere

Web paste, PWA share-sheet, browser extension, bulk import, or IaaS API. Five inputs, one graph.

Graph + vector store

Neo4j for typed relationships and traversal. Qdrant for semantic similarity. Together they answer questions Perplexity can’t.

Two surfaces, one backend

recome.app for consumers. /v1 for other companies. Same intelligence stack — double the leverage.

“Perplexity for what you’ve already looked at.” — the one-line pitch. Whether anyone wants this is the real question; everything else is execution.

Knowledge graph

A typed graph of your attention.

Nodes are topics, pages, entities, and you. Edges are typed (VIEWED, MENTIONS, HAS_INTENT, RELATED_TO) so traversal answers real questions, not just similarity.

:User :Topic :Page :Entity HAS_INTENT / VIEWED (solid) RELATED_TO (dashed)
Multi-tenant isolation

One graph. Strict isolation by construction.

Every IaaS write composes the userId as tenant_<tid>__<extUid>. Qdrant uses a separate collection per tenant. A cross-tenant query would need code that explicitly reverses the composition — impossible by accident.

✓ Strict isolation by construction (regression-tested in tenantUserId.test.ts)
Active agents

Background workers that bring you things. Perplexity is reactive. Recome is proactive.

Six agents read your graph on a schedule or on capture events and surface forgotten captures, price drops, fresh content, and stalled decisions. Every output cites the signals it drew from. None of them takes irreversible action — recommend only. See ADR-0012.

Recall

SHIPPED

F-317

Trust constraints (binding)
  • No irreversible action. Every agent output is a recommendation. User clicks buy / post / send / delete.
  • Sources mandatory. Every event cites the captured signals it drew from.
  • One-click pause per agent. Pause persists. No re-enable without explicit user action.
  • Quiet hours respected (default 22:00–08:00 local).
  • Daily cost ceiling per user (default $0.10 LLM spend, hard cap).
  • Personality Mirror = opt-in. Wipeable in one click. No psychometric labels. Never visible outside the user's own view.

Tenant isolation

Per-tenant Qdrant collections + composed userIds in Neo4j. Cross-tenant leaks blocked by construction, regression-tested.

Quota + metering

Per-month signal + query counters, 429 on overage with resetsAt. Stripe Meters reports daily for billing.

GDPR delete

DELETE /v1/users/:id drops everything: Neo4j namespace, Qdrant points, Postgres rows. Idempotent.

Unique surfaces

Eight features no competitor can ship without invalidating their core product.

Every surface below has a structural reason why Perplexity, Anthropic, Glean, Karma, Mem, and the agentic browsers can’t ship it from where they sit today. Full per-competitor argument in 0014.

F-401 · Mind Map

SPEC

Your own interest graph rendered spatially. Topic nodes sized by signal count, colored by cluster, edges by co-occurrence. Time-scrub the past 90 days.

Why structurally unique: Perplexity/Claude store opaque text memory. Mem renders note-graphs (links between things you typed). Karma has a wishlist, not a graph. We render YOUR graph back to YOU.

F-402 · Recall

SHIPPED

“You looked at this 21 days ago — still relevant.” Surfaces forgotten captures at the moment of re-engagement on the same topic.

Why structurally unique: Perplexity Memory only knows what you asked Perplexity. Comet only knows what happened in Comet. Mem only knows what you typed. We have the cross-platform temporal graph.

F-403 · Cross-platform Concierge

SPEC

You land on an Amazon page. We surface: “Three weeks ago this YouTube reviewer said this exact model has bad bearings. There’s a $200-cheaper alternative on Wayfair you also viewed.”

Why structurally unique: Karma explicitly does not collect browsing history (their architectural ceiling). Honey doesn’t see content sites. Amazon’s own recommender would never tell you “don’t buy this.”

F-404 · Curriculum mode

SPEC

“I want to understand climate policy.” → A 4-week reading list where each week bridges from a topic you already know to a new concept, using creators and platforms you already engage with.

Why structurally unique: Coursera/Khan personalize the destination. We personalize the BRIDGE from your existing graph to a learning goal.

F-405 · Interest-Graph-as-API

FOUNDATION SHIPPED

Other developers POST signals on behalf of their users, GET trails. rk_live_* bearer auth, per-tenant quotas, Stripe Meters. Same backend as recome.app.

Why structurally unique: Glean is enterprise-only ($7.2B valuation on that motion). Perplexity/Anthropic sell answers, not graphs. People Data Labs sells contact records — wrong category.

F-406 · Decision Completion

SPEC

“You’ve been comparing standing desks for 23 days. You evaluated three. The Reddit consensus you saw said Uplift is the safer choice for >5 years. Want me to walk through your evidence?”

Why structurally unique: Chat products have no temporal stall detection. Amazon would never tell you “you’re overthinking this.” We have the cross-session graph.

F-407 · Attention Composition

SPEC · OPT-IN

Weekly: “38% career/craft, 22% home setup, 18% news—up 12% w/w. You haven’t touched fitness in 6 weeks. Want to set a target?” Descriptive shape, NOT personality inference.

Hard framing constraints: Mirror, not judgment. No psychometric labels. Single-button wipe. Never shown to anyone but you.

F-408 · The Audit Log of You

FOUNDATION SHIPPED

Every signal we have. Every agent run. Every event surfaced. Every IaaS tenant that touched your data. Per-row delete. Full JSON export. Single button to wipe everything.

Why structurally unique: Every other memory product deliberately obscures this. We make it the loud feature. Marketing isn’t trust theatre — it’s the design.

Anti-features (will not build)
  • Personality type quiz. Big Five / MBTI inference from browsing is scientifically weak and privacy-hostile. Composition (F-407) is descriptive, not diagnostic.
  • Auto-purchase / auto-post / auto-anything irreversible. User clicks every action button.
  • Selling aggregate user data. Even anonymised. Closes the IaaS conversation forever.
  • Engagement-optimised feed. We surface specific cited items, not infinite scrolls.
  • Email / calendar replacement. Comet and OS providers own that. We stay in the interest-graph lane.
  • A browser. We work with whatever browser you use. Plus mobile. Plus the PWA share-sheet.
  • Always-on screen / audio capture. Limitless tried; Meta absorbed them; capture disabled Dec 2025. Different value model from ours.

Differentiation

We do not compete with Perplexity. We complement it.

Perplexity searches the world. Recome searches your past attention. Different category, different data, different value.

Question Perplexity / ChatGPT Recome
What is X? ✓ Excellent — world knowledge, fresh. ✗ Wrong tool. We route to Perplexity and say so.
What was I researching last Tuesday? ✗ No data. Cannot answer. ✓ Trail-windowed retrieval over signals.
I’m about to buy this — does it conflict with anything I learned? ✗ No record of what you saw. ✓ Trail intersection + LLM judgment over captures.
Summarize my [topic] research across all platforms last 3 weeks. ✗ No cross-platform signal. ✓ Multi-source summarization, dwell-weighted.
Find the YouTube reviewer who said brand X has bad bearings. ✗ Cannot retrieve from your history. ✓ Entity-disambig + fine-grained retrieval.
I’ve been on the fence about [thing] — decide for me. ✗ Generic answer; no context on what you considered. ✓ Preference modeling + opinion synthesis from your trail.
The risk is real: with weak capture, Recome is a worse Perplexity in week 1. The product only feels magical in week 2–3 once the graph is dense. That’s why bulk-import on day 1 is the load-bearing retention bet in Phase 1, not Phase 2.
