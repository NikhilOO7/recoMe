Core Capabilities

What the Extension Does

Four interconnected systems that run entirely in the browser — no data ever leaves the device.

🧠

Feed Intelligence

Filters content across 8 platforms based on your personal preference graph — not keyword blocklists. The filter learns what you genuinely care about and dims everything else without removing it.

Platforms: Amazon · YouTube · Twitter · Instagram · Facebook · LinkedIn · eBay · Walmart
🕸️

Interest Graph Builder

Every scroll, click, and dwell time writes to a weighted entity graph stored locally. Entities are connected by typed edges — interested_in, blocked, related_to — with numeric weights that decay or strengthen over time.

e.g. interested_in[Rust, w:0.91] · blocked[crypto, w:0.95]
💡

Inline Insights

OpportunityRanker scores every item on the current page and surfaces the top 3 insights as non-intrusive inline cards — directly in the platform you're already browsing, no tab switching required.

Signals: relevance · evidence quality · urgency · novelty vs past views
🔄

Multi-Platform Sync

One interest graph spans all 8 platforms. A preference learned from your LinkedIn browsing affects Amazon recommendations. Affinity propagation automatically extends your known interests to adjacent topics without explicit user action.

Graph sync: device-local · optional encrypted backup · never sold

Big Tech Positioning

The hyperscalers have built the most sophisticated interest graphs in history. They just serve them to advertisers, not users.

Browsing-only. No cross-platform graph.

Browsing history stays in the browser. There is no interest graph — just a URL list. Cannot observe what you do on Amazon or Twitter. Cannot surface insights. Cannot filter feeds. Passive storage, not intelligence.

YouTube Algorithm
Engagement-optimized. Platform's interests, not yours.

YouTube has a sophisticated watch-time graph. It is calibrated to maximize session length for Google's ad revenue — not to surface what you actually want to see. The graph is locked inside YouTube and optimized against you.

Meta (Facebook / Instagram)
Interest graph sold to advertisers. Locked in ecosystem.

Meta's interest profile is the most detailed behavioral graph on the internet. It is not available to you — only to advertisers. It cannot leave Meta's ecosystem, cannot inform your Amazon searches, cannot help you on LinkedIn. It is your data, used without you.

Honest Assessment

Critical Risks

Three real risks, each with a mitigation strategy. We'd rather surface these clearly than have an investor discover them post-investment.

⚠️
Platform DOM Changes

Amazon, Twitter, and other platforms update their HTML structure without warning. When they do, the relevant adapter breaks until it is patched. This is the most operationally intensive ongoing risk. Mitigation: automated DOM-change monitoring CI/CD pipeline, fast-patch release cadence (target: <24h patch turnaround), adapter modular design so one platform breaking does not affect others.

⚠️
Browser Policy Changes (MV3 Restrictions)

Chrome's Manifest V3 transition restricts certain extension capabilities — particularly around persistent background scripts and network request interception. Some planned capabilities may require architecture changes to remain compliant. Mitigation: MV3-first architecture already implemented. Monitoring W3C and browser vendor working groups. Firefox as secondary distribution channel maintains flexibility if Chrome restrictions tighten further.

⚠️
Cold-Start UX

For the first 3–5 browsing sessions, the interest graph is sparse. The filter doesn't do much. Inline insights are limited. New users experience a "why am I paying for this?" moment before the graph is rich enough to deliver value. Mitigation: onboarding flow that shows graph-building progress visually, optional interest seed questionnaire, explicit Day-1 vs Week-2 expectation setting in the UI, free tier gives users time to see value before paying.
