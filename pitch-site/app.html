Critical Risks

Known Risks. Concrete Mitigations.

We've identified three primary risks and have active mitigations for each.

⚠️
Mobile engagement competition

TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube all have massive engagement loops and near-infinite content moats. RecoMe App is used differently — intentionally, not addictively — but discovery is hard when competing for home screen space. Mitigation: web-first approach reduces friction, and the use case (knowing yourself) doesn't compete with entertainment.

⚠️
App Store distribution and 30% cut

Apple's 30% in-app purchase fee materially impacts subscription economics for a $9/month product. Mitigation: web app as primary product with native app as a convenience layer. Subscriptions managed via web checkout to avoid the App Store fee on the majority of revenue.

⚠️
Graph quality without the extension

The app is significantly more powerful when fed by the browser extension. Without it, the graph starts cold and preference quality is lower. Mitigation: manual preference import, Vivino and Spotify data import at onboarding, and a structured onboarding questionnaire that seeds the graph with meaningful initial data.
