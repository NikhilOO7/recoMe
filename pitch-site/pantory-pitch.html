Three things the SDK does.
No jargon, no fluff.

The RecoMe SDK is a backend service Pantory calls when a patient uploads a report. Here's exactly what happens — explained like you're talking to a 10-year-old.

SDK Components — In Plain English
Four building blocks.
Here's what each one actually does.

These are the four parts of the SDK. Each has a technical name, but we'll explain what it actually does in language that makes sense.

Interest Graph
James's Medical Memory
"Like a doctor who remembers every conversation you've ever had with them"

Every time James reads a report section, clicks to learn more, or re-reads something, we record it. We build a map of everything he cares about — his tumor marker, his treatment progress, his vascular involvement. Each topic has a "weight" — how much he cares about it — and that weight grows each session. By visit 4, we know James better than a doctor who's only met him once.

OpportunityRanker
The "What Matters Most" Filter
"Like a triage nurse who looks at 40 test results and says: call about these 3"

A typical CT report has 12–15 findings. Most are stable background noise. The ranker scores every single finding using four questions: Does James care about this? Is it actionable? Is it urgent? Is it new? Findings below the threshold get filtered out silently. James sees only what he needs to act on — ranked from most to least important.

Memory Pack
The "Skip What You Know" Tracker
"Like a teacher who doesn't re-teach the same lesson twice"

We explained what CA 19-9 is in session 1. We know James re-read it twice, so he understands it now. In session 4, we don't re-explain it from scratch — we skip straight to "here's what your number means today." The Memory Pack tracks every term, explanation, and concept James has already encountered across all his sessions.

Profile Extractor
The Medical Report Reader
"Like a medical scribe that turns 8 pages of doctor-speak into structured data"

Before any of the other pieces can work, someone has to read the PDF and pull out the facts — tumor size: 3.1cm, CA 19-9: 312, bilirubin: 1.8. The Profile Extractor does this using specialty modules for oncology, cardiology, and labs. It also computes what changed since last time — "CA 19-9 dropped 52% since cycle 3" — which no single-document LLM can do.
