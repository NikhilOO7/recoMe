The Problem

Every app starts from zero.
Your preferences die at the tab.

Every social feed, e-commerce site, and AI assistant treats you as a stranger every time you arrive. Your interest signals are trapped inside platform silos, invisible to the apps that could use them, and invisible to you.

🔒

Preference Data is Imprisoned

Amazon knows your shopping patterns. YouTube knows your viewing habits. LinkedIn knows your career trajectory. None of them talk to each other — and none of them tell you.

🔄

Cold Start Never Ends

Every new app asks you to start over. There is no portable preference identity. Developers spend months building personalization infrastructure that's been solved before.

🎯

Zero Transparency

You can't see your own interest graph. You can't edit it. You can't take it with you. Recommendations are delivered from a black box you neither own nor control.

The Insight

Preferences are behavioral infrastructure, not product features. The moment any app can call a single API and receive a structured, weighted interest graph for any user — cold start disappears, personalization becomes a commodity, and the user finally owns their identity. That's the layer we're building.

Risk Analysis

Known risks, concrete mitigations

High Platform DOM Fragility
Risk
Amazon or YouTube updates their DOM, breaking selector-based signal capture.
Mitigation
Automated selector monitoring with <2hr patch window. GenericAdapter fallback captures signals even when specific adapters fail. Alert system triggers on >10% signal drop per platform.
High Big Tech Competition
Risk
Google or Apple builds a native preference layer into Chrome/iOS.
Mitigation
18–24 month structural window — their graphs are siloed by design. Compliance certifications (HIPAA/SOC2) take 18+ months to replicate and block regulated enterprise deals. Decentralized architecture is antithetical to their business models.
Medium Cold Start Problem
Risk
New users have no graph data. Personalization is weak until sufficient signals accumulate.
Mitigation
One-click data imports from Spotify, Netflix, YouTube, Reddit. Onboarding questionnaire seeds 15+ initial nodes. Vertical-calibrated defaults (healthcare, legal) provide instant relevance. Value visible from Day 1 via feed filtering.
Medium Enterprise Sales Cycle
Risk
Healthcare enterprise deals take 6–12 months. Cash flow risk in Year 1.
Mitigation
Self-serve Growth tier ($499/mo) generates revenue immediately. Pantory reference customer reduces deal cycles by 60%. SDK starter tier ($49/mo) creates a bottom-up land-and-expand motion that doesn't require enterprise procurement.
Medium SDK Commoditization
Risk
OpenAI or AWS releases a competing personalization SDK.
Mitigation
2+ years of accumulated per-user graph data creates switching costs competitors can't replicate. Compliance moat. 100+ marketplace modules create ecosystem lock-in. Domain-specific vertical modules (oncology, legal research) aren't built by general AI providers.
Medium Consumer Retention
Risk
Consumer users churn before the graph becomes valuable enough to retain them.
Mitigation
Graph value compounds — early churners leave a weaker product than they arrived with. ShopperAgent alerts create habitual re-engagement. Weekly digest emails surface graph insights. Team plans increase switching cost by adding social graph layer.
