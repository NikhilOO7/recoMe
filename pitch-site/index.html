Why 2026

The Window Is Open.
It Won't Stay Open Forever.

Four forces converged in 2025 to make this the exact right moment.

Force 1 · 2024–2025

AI-native apps are stateless — all of them

10,000+ AI SaaS products shipped in 2024–2025. Every single one resets on each session. They've acquired users and are hitting retention walls at session 2–3. The personalization layer is the #1 unsolved product problem right now.

Force 2 · Infrastructure

Graph database costs dropped 10× since 2022

Cloud-native graph databases made per-user preference graphs economically viable for a $49/month SDK. Running this at scale cost $50K/month in 2022. Today it costs under $2K. The unit economics only just became viable.

Force 3 · Regulation

Cookie deprecation creates a vacuum

Google's Privacy Sandbox eliminated third-party cookies in 2024. The entire ad-tech personalization stack is broken. Publishers, e-commerce, and content platforms are desperately looking for a first-party preference alternative. That's exactly what RecoMe provides.

Force 4 · Competition

Hyperscalers haven't noticed this layer

AWS Personalize does product recommendations. OpenAI memory is chat-only. Google's interest graph is locked inside Google. There is an 18–24 month window before a hyperscaler ships a competing product. That window is the market entry opportunity.

Force 5 · User Rights

Users want to own their preference data

The Apple privacy narrative, GDPR enforcement, and backlash against algorithmic feeds have created mainstream demand for user-controlled personalization. RecoMe is the first product built around the user owning their own taste identity.

The Verdict

5 forces. 1 company. Zero competitors.

The technology is built. The market gap is verified. The first customer (Pantory) is mapped. The window is 18–24 months before hyperscalers notice. This is the moment.
